# Incremental roadmap

## Slice 0 — merged

Engine skeleton, documented tables, village loop, isometric client, session server.

## Slice 1 — a stable housing block (this PR, with Slice 2)

- 2×2 house merging with map merge bits
- Entertainment: actor colony → theater show → coverage walker
- Education and health walkers (school, library, academy, barber, baths, hospital)
- Desirability objects (statues, plazas, governor's house)
- Water overlay pipe grid
- Right-click speech in C3 phrasing (“These people need a temple.”)

## Slice 2 — industry and trade (this PR)

- Clay / timber / olives / vines / iron / marble
- Workshops (pottery, furniture, oil, wine) and warehouses with accepting / getting / not-accepting orders
- Empire map: Capua (land), Tarentum and Syracusae (sea). Open a route, staff a trade post or dock, caravans buy and sell.
- Markets deliver pottery and other goods; houses consume them.

## Slice 3 — city ratings and career

- Culture, prosperity, peace, favor
- Requests from Caesar
- Sentiment, crime, riots
- *A Village is Born* win condition (150 people) then Brundisium

## Slice 4 — military and disasters

- Forts, barracks, wolves, invasions
- Earthquakes, collapsing buildings as scripted events

## Slice 5 — Pharaoh ruleset

Floodplain, water table, festival walkers, Egyptian housing — same engine, different data. Only after C3 blocks feel right.
